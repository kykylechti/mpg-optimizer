# mpg-optimizer

## Goal 

This project aims to provide the best team available for a MPG mercato. 

## What is MPG ?

MPG is a french app featuring leagues in which you can play with your friends competing accross real football league.
You start by selecting a league (in my example I play in Ligue 1), and when the season starts the mercato begins : 

1. You select a list of players playing in ligue 1 with a budget of 500 billions euros, each player having a fixed minimum cost you have to spend to earn it. 
2. The first turn ends, you receive the players on which your bid was the highest and take back the money spent on players you didn't get.
3. Another turn begins.

The bidings ends after a week or when no one has any euro left.

Each week you face an opponent among the players in your league. Before the beginning of the gameweek in the real football league, you have to select the player you want to put in the starting lineup. Accross the gameweek, your players will obtain notes according to their statistics and the goal they score will be scored in the game too. 

At the end of the gameweek, every players whose notes are high enough to go through the ennemy team scores if they didn't score a real goal.
